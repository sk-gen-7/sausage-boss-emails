Cook Like a Boss

Your sausages are almost there. Here's how to make them legendary.

For the Smoker

250°F until 160°F internal. Low and slow wins.

For the Grill

Medium-low heat until 165°F. Don't rush greatness.

From Frozen

Thaw in fridge overnight or cook from frozen (add 5 min).

Jalapeno Popper Sausage Boat

Slice your sausage lengthwise, stuff with cream cheese and jalapenos, wrap in bacon, and smoke until golden. A showstopper every time.
